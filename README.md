## 🎯 What is OSINT?

**Open Source Intelligence (OSINT)** is the collection and analysis of information from publicly available sources. This includes:

- Public records and government databases
- Social media platforms and online profiles
- News articles and publications
- Academic papers and research
- Corporate filings and business records
- Geospatial data and satellite imagery
- And much more...

> ⚠️ **Ethical Notice**: This repository is intended for legitimate research, journalism, cybersecurity, and educational purposes only. Always respect privacy laws, terms of service, and ethical guidelines when conducting OSINT research.
